## T50 in detail: the strongest evidence of an edge in this project so far
| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| DEV 2019–2022 | 112 trades, **+1.24R** net (95% lower bound +0.27R), profit factor 3.7, positive on 6 of 9 coins |
| VAL 2023–2024 (seen for the family, not for these rules) | 62 trades, **+1.44R** (lower bound +0.44R). Every coin positive, but no coin reached the 10-trade minimum the breadth rule needs. |
| **Holdout: 16 coins never used, 2019–2024** | **276 trades, +0.74R** (lower bound +0.37R), profit factor 2.6, positive on 13 of 15 eligible coins (87%). Includes delisted coins and coins that fell 60–80%. |
| Walk-forward 2021 / 2022 / 2023 / 2024 | +1.89R / **−0.80R** / +0.05R / +1.27R → 3 of 4 positive |
| Random-entry benchmark (200 runs, same exits and stops, DEV+VAL) | T50 **+1.31R** vs random mean **+0.52R** (95th percentile +0.61R). T50 beat all 200 runs. |
| Costs | Costs are only 0.03–0.05R per trade. At 0.30%/side fees, VAL is still +1.39R. |

**What it means:**
- **The entry timing adds value.** T50 beats random entries by about +0.8R per trade. It is also positive on unseen coins.
- **The exit is half the story.** Random entries with the same exits still make +0.52R in this period: cutting losers and riding winners pays in a rising market.
- **Sample period, the main risk:** 2019–2024 contains two large bull markets. The only bear year available (2022) lost −0.80R per trade, and the 2025–26 bear window is consumed. **There is no clean historical test of a prolonged bear market.**
- **Dependence on big winners:** results rely on rare large winners (the five biggest DEV trades are +14R to +54R). The win rate is about 42%, and most trades lose about 0.7–1R.
- **Trade count:** a daily system on 9 coins produces about 30 trades a year, so the 100-trade VAL rule cannot be met on 2 years of this universe.
- **Capital efficiency is low under the Conservative limits.**
  - With 0.5% risk per trade and a 3-ATR stop (median 17% away), each position is about 3% of capital.
  - The 9-coin portfolio made **+7.8% over DEV**, with a 5.1% max drawdown and a Sharpe of about 0.7. Buy-and-hold averaged +421%, but with drawdowns of 70%+.
  - A genuine edge in R does not yet mean meaningful returns. Changing sizing is a separate decision for the user. It has not been changed.

## RS3
- Positive everywhere but weaker: DEV +0.53R, VAL +0.35R (lower bound +0.03R), holdout +0.31R on 210 trades.
- It beat all 200 random-basket runs (+0.45R vs the 95th percentile +0.23R).
- It fails the count and breadth rules, and lost −0.74R in 2022.
- Its portfolio drawdown is tiny (0.6%) because exposure is low (≈ 13%).

## Reasons the original strategies failed, now confirmed
1. **Wrong timeframe:** 1h signals have no gross edge, and costs are 15% of R.
2. **Exits cut winners early:** the 2-ATR trailing stop and fixed 3-ATR take-profit cap the fat right tail that makes trend following work. T50's edge comes from that tail (avg winner +3.9R).
3. **Tight stops get hit by noise:** a third of trades stopped out within 2 bars.
